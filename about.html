About SeamFlow

Systems, Not Just Advice.

SeamFlow Consulting is a business operations excellence consultancy dedicated to helping healthcare SMEs in Nigeria build the systems and structures they need to operate effectively, consistently, profitably and sustainably.

Akunna Mbanefo Connect on LinkedIn
Our Founder

Led by Akunna Mbanefo, Principal Consultant.

Akunna’s journey into healthcare operations began on the ground—not in a boardroom.

For approximately three years, she worked in HMO marketing across Nigeria’s Southeast, enrolling clients, liaising directly with healthcare providers, and helping ensure that insured patients could access the healthcare services they had enrolled to receive—not simply hold a card that promised access.

She also managed operations at an eye hospital, where she led the process of bringing HMOs into the facility for inspection and accreditation. Under her leadership, the hospital successfully onboarded three separate HMOs as approved healthcare partners.

Alongside her healthcare management experience, Akunna spent time on radio promoting preventive eye health awareness within the community, encouraging people to undergo routine checks that could help detect conditions such as glaucoma and cataract before they became emergencies.

This combination of experience—facilitating healthcare access, managing the day-to-day operations of a healthcare facility, and developing the compliance relationships required to maintain access to HMO business—forms the foundation of SeamFlow Consulting.

At SeamFlow Consulting, we design repeatable operational systems that help healthcare facilities move from being chaotic and owner-dependent to becoming structured, scalable and consistently profitable.

No unnecessary complexity. No jargon. Just structure that works.

If your healthcare facility feels disorganized or overwhelming behind the scenes, it’s time for a positive change.

Our Mission

Every Healthcare SME in Nigeria Should Run Like a Real Institution.

We believe that a small, privately owned clinic deserves the opportunity to demonstrate the same level of operational excellence as a well-structured and well-managed teaching hospital.

That means

  • Clearly defined processes
  • Documented SOPs
  • Measured performance
  • A trained workforce
  • A facility that patients can trust and remain loyal to

Our mission is to help make this level of operational excellence achievable for healthcare SMEs across Nigeria.

Our Methodology

Clarity. Structure. Growth.™

We follow the same three-stage sequence in every engagement because a facility cannot skip one stage and expect the next stage to hold.

Stage 1

Diagnose the Real Gaps

We begin with a Business Systems Audit across the facility’s departments.

The audit provides an honest, scored picture of where structure already exists and where it is missing—before a single SOP is written.

Stage 2

Install the Missing Structure

Once the gaps are identified, we develop the structure required to address them.

Every department with operational challenges receives documented processes, SOPs and workflows aligned with the Nigerian regulatory bodies responsible for governing those areas.

Stage 3

Keep It Alive

The objective is not just to create systems, but to ensure that they continue to work.

Performance measurement, a well-trained and vibrant workforce, strategies for appropriate brand positioning, an expansion roadmap and other growth-focused interventions help turn the new structure into a lasting standard rather than a one-time project.
